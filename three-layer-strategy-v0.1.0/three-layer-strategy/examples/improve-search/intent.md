# Intent: Improve editorial search

Status: accepted
Owner: Product team

## User or stakeholder

Journalists searching the editorial archive.

## Problem observed

Exact title matches are sometimes buried below partial body-text matches, increasing the time needed to find a known article.

## Evidence

Three support cases and two observed search sessions show repeated query reformulation for known titles.

## Desired outcome

For a known article title, journalists can find the expected article in the first results without changing the query.

## Non-goals

- redesigning the search interface;
- semantic or generative search;
- changing access permissions.

## Constraints

Existing permission filtering and result latency must be preserved.

## Open questions

None for the first increment.
