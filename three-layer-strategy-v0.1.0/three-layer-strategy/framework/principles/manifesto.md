# The Three-Layer Manifesto

Software is becoming easier to produce. Useful software is not becoming automatic.

The scarce resource is no longer only the ability to write code. It is the ability to choose the right problem, express intent, preserve context, make trade-offs and verify what was built.

We believe:

- AI expands who can contribute to a product.
- Every contributor should be able to submit an idea through a shared, structured path.
- Human, assisted and agentic work are three valid modes, not three rungs on a ladder.
- The right question is always: who should perform this step, under which constraints, and how will the result be checked?
- Product truth belongs in versioned artifacts, not in private conversations with a model.
- Autonomy must grow with observability, reversibility and evidence.
- Open formats and portable instructions are healthier than dependence on one model or vendor.
- Code generation is not completion. A change is complete only when its intended outcome has been verified.
- Compute, tokens, attention and review time are finite resources. Good engineering spends all four deliberately.

Our goal is not maximal automation. It is the smallest responsible combination of human judgment and machine capability that delivers a valuable, verifiable outcome.
