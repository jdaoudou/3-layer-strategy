# AI usage policy

- Disclose material AI contribution according to the host organization's rules.
- Do not represent generated output as verified evidence.
- Preserve provenance for important requirements and decisions.
- Never fabricate user research, logs, citations, test results or approvals.
- Use human review where outputs affect rights, safety, employment, finance, privacy or public information.
- Prefer open, portable artifacts over model-specific memory.
- Record any model limitation that materially affects residual risk.
