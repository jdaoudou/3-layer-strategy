# Choose a layer for a step

## Method

1. Name the exact step and deliverable.
2. Inspect its assessment scores and any new evidence.
3. Choose the lowest-control mode that remains responsible and verifiable.
4. State the human control point.
5. For agentic mode, state permissions, stop conditions and independent checks.
6. Record the decision in `assessment.md`.

Changing layer mid-workflow is expected when uncertainty or risk changes. Do not treat the first selection as permanent.
